## Decisions and deviations

- **Codec alignment check (§6.5 step 11):** the decoded onset is verified by the lag that maximises the
  correlation of the first differences of source and decoded region over 10 ms from 1 ms before the onset
  (must be 0 ± 1 frame). A threshold-crossing detector on the decoded signal is useless with 60 ms Opus frames
  (pre-echo and attack smearing move it 2–90 frames while the timing is in fact preserved).
- **Unit streams** end with a 40 ms silent tail as well (the codec's look-ahead lands in silence).
- **Stub kit:** instrument `stub` uses the grand's §3.7 damper/free T60 formulas; `lastDamper` 88; HARD, one
  layer (velRef 64); `gainDb` −6 dB (natural peak −9 dBFS); `embeddedRoomDb` +30 dB (dry synthesis),
  `embeddedEdtS` 0; `releaseRule` {0, 0.7, 3 s, 0.25, −9 dB}; version fixed `2026.09.1` for determinism.
  Region key ranges: root ± 1, the outermost extended to 0 and 127.
- **Upright `lastDamper` evidence:** a release "rings on" when its early-decay T60 > max(1 s, 2 × the damped
  formula) on a root ≥ 60 (the upright formula itself exceeds 1 s below ≈ key 30).
- **Release early-decay fits** use a 2.5 ms RMS envelope (a 0.1 s T60 spans only two 10 ms blocks in 12 dB).
- **Level corrections** (§3.5, clamped ±2.5 dB) are baked into each sustain region's `gainDb`; `levelCurve`
  holds the per-layer centres (median over roots, then isotonic).
- **"The una corda C4"** for `export_test_regions.py`: Salamander has no una corda recordings, so it is the
  region the grand plays for C4 v64 (HD layer v8); the engine's soft bus acts on it.
- **Catalogue tuning override** is `null` or `{aHz, temperament}` with the `Temperament` enum name.
- `assets/licenses/SOURCES.csv` is excluded from the ledger scan (it is the ledger's published copy).
