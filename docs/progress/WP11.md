## Decisions and deviations

- **Codec alignment check (§6.5 step 11):** the decoded onset is verified by the lag that maximises the
  correlation of the first differences of source and decoded region over 10 ms from 1 ms before the onset
  (must be 0 ± 1 frame). A threshold-crossing detector on the decoded signal is useless with 60 ms Opus frames
  (pre-echo and attack smearing move it 2–90 frames while the timing is in fact preserved).
- **Unit streams** end with a 40 ms silent tail as well (the codec's look-ahead lands in silence).
- **Stub kit:** instrument `stub` uses the grand's §3.7 damper/free T60 formulas; `lastDamper` 88; HARD, one
  layer (velRef 64); `gainDb` −6 dB (natural peak −9 dBFS); `embeddedRoomDb` +30 dB (dry synthesis),
  `embeddedEdtS` 0; `releaseRule` {0, 0.7, 3 s, 0.25, −9 dB}; version fixed `2026.09.1` for determinism.
  Region key ranges: root ± 1, the outermost extended to 0 and 127.
- **Upright `lastDamper` evidence:** a release "rings on" when its early-decay T60 > max(1 s, 2 × the damped
  formula) on a root ≥ 60 (the upright formula itself exceeds 1 s below ≈ key 30).
- **Release early-decay fits** use a 2.5 ms RMS envelope (a 0.1 s T60 spans only two 10 ms blocks in 12 dB).
- **Level corrections** (§3.5, clamped ±2.5 dB) are baked into each sustain region's `gainDb`; `levelCurve`
  holds the per-layer centres (median over roots, then isotonic).
- **"The una corda C4"** for `export_test_regions.py`: Salamander has no una corda recordings, so it is the
  region the grand plays for C4 v64 (HD layer v8); the engine's soft bus acts on it.
- **Catalogue tuning override** is `null` or `{aHz, temperament}` with the `Temperament` enum name.
- `assets/licenses/SOURCES.csv` is excluded from the ledger scan (it is the ledger's published copy).

## Stage 2 decisions and deviations

- **Alignment check window 20 ms** (was 10 ms) with a plateau rule (lag 0 accepted when its correlation is
  within 1% of the best) and, for regions with no sharp attack (a release that starts on the sounding tone),
  a 0.5 s window. Salamander F#1 v1 read +4 frames at 10 ms and 0 at 20 ms; an upright release read 48
  (one period of the tone). A real shift of an attack is still caught (tested at −3, +2, +5 frames).
- **Top octave pitch** (root ≥ 96): when the partial fit is missing or its first partial disagrees with the
  fundamental's own spectral peak (±7%) by > 10 cents, the peak gives the pitch and B is capped at 0.003.
  Salamander's C8 sounds ≈ +95 cents on every layer (the ±3% partial search had locked onto noise and read
  −60). The spectral floor is now relative to the strongest peak above 0.5·f0 (sub-50 Hz rumble set it
  before, so treble upright notes found no partials); treble notes ≥ 90 are measured over 0.05–0.55 s. B is
  kept ≥ 0 inside the fit (a negative intermediate B crashed the upright build).
- **Damper T60 fits**: VCSL's release samples (`_Far` takes) are room-dominated; the fastest take per root is
  used, a root outside 0.5–2× the §3.7 formula falls back to the formula with a report line (instead of
  failing the build), and when fewer than half the roots are in range the formula is used for every key.
  Result: upright 2 of 19 roots in range, harpsichord 13 of 32 → both kits use the §3.7 formula. The report
  lists every measured value and knee for L-3.
- **Grand Railsback**: the raw fit gives A0 −20.1, C8 +11.1 c (C8 just under +15); `tune_nat` gives the
  same (it is 0 at the top), so the raw fit is used and the warning is in the report for L-3.
- **Kit directory = instrument id** (`instruments/grand/`), as stage 1's tool already did; only `grand-hd` is
  built (samples-hd approved), `grand-std` is not shipped.
- **Catalogue**: Sankey and Bednarek ship some works as one file (Goldberg, suites, partitas, K. 281,
  op. 126): one movement each. No `expectDurationSec` values were typed (the site listings are not in the
  cache), so the duration check has nothing to compare; every other §4.7 check runs. Sources `bednarek`
  (CC0, performed), `nieb` (CC BY-SA 3.0), `frantz` (PD); Mutopia is tier B at source level with
  work-level tier C on K. 457 and op. 111 i (the §4.7 L-6 list). Empty shelves are dropped.
- **Ledger licence ids**: the manifest's `CC0` and `CC BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL` map to `CC0-1.0` and
  `CC-BY-SA-3.0`.
- **Harpsichord seams**: 4′ regions borrowed for the top 8′ keys get `seamGainDb` +4.5 to +17.6 dB (the 4′
  rank is much quieter); flagged for L-3 listening, not clamped.
